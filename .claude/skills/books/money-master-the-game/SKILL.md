## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### The 7 Simple Steps to Financial Freedom
1. **Commit to savings** — Automatic "money machine" saving system, increase by 1% every 6 months
2. **Know the rules** — Shatter the 9 financial myths that destroy wealth (fees, broker conflicts, etc.)
3. **Dream price & plan** — Calculate your financial freedom number, create realistic timeline
4. **Asset allocation** — Three-bucket system: Security (40-60%), Risk/Growth (30-50%), Dream (5-10%)
5. **Lifetime income** — Create income that lasts as long as you live (annuities, Social Security, systematic withdrawal)
6. **Learn from .001%** — Master interviews: Buffett, Dalio, Icahn, Bogle, Swensen, Paul Tudor Jones, etc.
7. **Enjoy & share** — Secret to living is giving; gratitude and contribution create fulfillment

Use this as overall framework; each step has detailed implementation in chapters 1-7.

### Three-Bucket System (Asset Allocation)
- **Security Bucket** (40-60%): Bonds, annuities, CDs, pensions, Treasury securities — lifetime income foundation
- **Risk/Growth Bucket** (30-50%): Stocks, REITs, real estate, growth investments — beat inflation, long-term growth
- **Dream Bucket** (5-10%): Higher-risk opportunities with potential for large gains — fulfill life dreams

*Ch 4.1-4.3*

### All Seasons Strategy (Ray Dalio)
Performs in any economic environment through specific allocation:
- **Stocks** 30% — growth in economic expansion
- **Long-term Treasuries** 40% — deflation protection
- **Intermediate-term Treasuries** 15% — recession protection
- **Gold** 7.5% — inflation protection
- **Commodities** 7.5% — inflation protection

Based on four economic seasons: Growth, Inflation, Deflation, Recession.
*Ch 5.1, 6.6*

### Defense First Principle
**Defense is 10x more important than offense.** Focus first on "don't lose," then on growth.

- **5-to-1 Rule**: Risk $1 to make $5 — allows 80% failure rate and still break even
- **200-Day Moving Average**: Primary trend indicator — exit when price drops below
- **Never be contrarian**: Align with predominant trend, don't fight it
- **Turtle wins race**: Stay in game as long as possible through diversification and discipline

*Ch 6.0, 6.5*

### Index Fund Revolution (Jack Bogle, David Swensen)
**Investors as a group can't beat the market because they are the market.**

- **96% of active funds fail** to beat market even before behavioral mistakes
- **Cost drag destruction**: 2% all-in costs turn 7% market return into 5% — $1→$30 vs $1→$10 over 50 years
- **Reversion to mean**: Past performance doesn't predict future; star funds become average
- **Solution**: Low-cost index funds (<20 basis points) beat 90%+ of managers automatically

*Ch 2.1, 6.2, 6.3*

### Fee Impact Framework
Every 1% in fees reduces long-term wealth by approximately 33%.

| Fee Rate | Starting | Final Value (40 years) | Lost to Fees |
|----------|---------|------------------------|--------------|
| 3% | $100K | $480K | $740K |
| 2% | $100K | $704K | $516K |
| 0.5% | $100K | $1.27M | $53K |

*Ch 2.2*

### 1-3-5 Formula (Speed Up Wealth)
Five accelerators for reaching financial freedom faster:
1. **Save More** — Increase savings rate gradually (1% every 6 months)
2. **Earn More** — Increase income, invest difference
3. **Reduce Fees & Taxes** — Tax-efficient strategies, low-cost funds
4. **Better Returns** — Proper asset allocation, avoid market timing
5. **Change Lifestyle** — Control expenses, avoid lifestyle inflation

*Ch 3.3-3.7*

### Tax-Efficient Investing Hierarchy
1. **401(k) with employer match** — Free money, contribute up to match
2. **Roth IRA** — Tax-free growth and withdrawals
3. **Traditional IRA** — Tax-deferred growth
4. **401(k) beyond match** — If low fees and good options
5. **HSA** — Triple tax advantage if eligible
6. **Taxable brokerage** — With tax-efficient index funds

*Ch 2.8, 6.4, 6.11*

### Compounding Power
$1 doubles every year for 20 years → $1,048,576 (tax-free)
With 33% annual taxes → $28,000

**Einstein called compound interest "the most important invention in all of human history."**

Start early: William vs. James example — James started 20 years later and never caught up despite investing 4x as much.

*Ch 1.3, 3.6*

### 4% Rule (Safe Withdrawal Rate)
Withdraw 4% of portfolio annually, adjusted for inflation, with high probability of not running out of money in 30+ years.

**Important**: Requires flexibility; may need to reduce withdrawals during market downturns or extend retirement period.

*Ch 5.4*

### Economic Seasons Framework
Four distinct market environments require different asset allocations:

| Season | Characteristics | Winning Assets |
|--------|----------------|----------------|
| Growth | Rising growth, moderate inflation | Stocks, Commodities |
| Inflation | Rising inflation, stagnant growth | Gold, Commodities, TIPS |
| Deflation | Falling prices, economic contraction | Long-term Treasuries |
| Recession | Falling growth, falling inflation | Intermediate-term Treasuries |

*Ch 5.1, 6.6*

### Limiting Beliefs Framework
Identify and replace mental patterns that constrain financial success:

**Common limiting beliefs**:
- "I'm not good with money"
- "Rich people are less happy"
- "Investing is too complicated"
- "I'll never be wealthy"
- "The market is rigged"

**Solution**: Question validity, replace with empowering beliefs, take consistent action.

*Ch 1.4*

### Three Decisions Framework
Every significant life/financial decision comes down to:
1. **What are you going to focus on?** (Focus = feeling)
2. **What does it mean?** (Meaning = emotion)
3. **What are you going to do?** (Action = destiny)

*Ch 7.3*

### Success Without Fulfillment
Achievement alone doesn't create happiness. Financial freedom without fulfillment leads to emptiness.

**Secret to living is giving**: Contribution and gratitude create lasting fulfillment.

*Ch 7.2, 7.3*
