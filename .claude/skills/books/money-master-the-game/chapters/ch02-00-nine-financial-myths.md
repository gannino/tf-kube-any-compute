# Chapter 2.0: Break Free: Shattering the 9 Financial Myths

## Core Idea
The financial system is riddled with "landmines"—myths and lies marketed to investors that systematically destroy wealth. This section shows you how to save $150,000-$1,000,000 over your investment lifetime.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Two-Phase Investment Journey**:
  1. **Accumulation Phase**: Socking away money for growth, climbing toward critical mass
  2. **Decumulation Phase**: Withdrawing income in retirement, skiing down the mountain enjoying the view
- **Critical Mass/Pinnacle**: The tipping point where investment growth generates enough income to support your lifestyle without working
- **Money Power Principle 1**: "Don't get in the game unless you know the rules!" — ignorance is pain, struggle, and giving your fortune away

## Key Concepts
- **$13T Mutual Fund Lie**: There's $13 trillion in actively managed mutual funds with 265 million account holders—90% of Americans participate in a strategy where they give up 60%+ of potential lifetime upside
- **The 2.5% Fee Disaster**: Jack Bogle's example—$10,000 at age 20 with 7% growth = $574,464 by age 80. With 2.5% fees = only $140,274. Fees eat 77% of your returns!
- **Failed 401(k) Experiment**: "This do-it-yourself pension system has failed" (Teresa Ghilarducci) — expects non-experts to achieve professional results while navigating intentionally complex, dangerous system
- **Brokers Not Fiduciaries**: Brokers work for companies not legally required to do what's in your best interest—funnel money to products most profitable for them/their firm
- **Game is Still Winnable**: Technology has made investing more efficient—click of a button, minimal charges, invest in anything anywhere. "It's easier than it's ever been to do pretty well"

## Mental Models
- **Casinos Stacked Deck**: Nearly all mutual fund companies are "ultimate casinos"—they capture you, you're going nowhere, and they're guaranteed revenue whether you win or lose
- **House Always Wins**: Steve Wynn's truth—casinos have edge but no guaranteed victory (high roller can take millions in one night). Mutual funds have stacked deck with guaranteed revenue
- **Risk from Not Knowing**: Warren Buffett's principle—risk comes from not knowing what you're doing
- **Mountain Climbing Metaphor**: Financial Freedom is your personal Mount Everest—requires preparation, understanding dangers, some known and some that sneak up like storms. Can't ask someone to climb it for you, but need guide with your best interests

## Anti-patterns
- **Financial Porn**: Sensationalized financial news, hot picks, five-star ratings—"luring us into glossy pages where centerfolds are swapped with five-star ratings"
- **Chasing Returns**: Big business that feeds on FOMO—advertisers fighting for your assets, creating feeling you're missing out
- **Past Performance Trap**: Mutual funds start multiple incubation funds, euthanize losers, market the one winner—creates illusion of skill (actually survivorship bias)
- **Herd Mentality**: Following emotions/broker recommendations—buy high when market up, sell low when emotional pain too much
