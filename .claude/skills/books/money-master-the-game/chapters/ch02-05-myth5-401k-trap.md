# Chapter 2.5: Myth 5: "Your Retirement Is Just a 401(k) Away"

## Core Idea
401(k) was designed as supplement to pensions, not replacement. Most plans charge 2.5%+ in combined fees (mutual funds + plan administration), eroding 50-70% of potential retirement—while low-cost plans like America's Best 401k offer total costs of 0.75%.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **401(k) Failed Experiment**: "Baby boomers have been the primary mice in the great 401(k) retirement experiment"—shifted all risk/expense from employer to employee
- **Six Impossible Requirements**: Teresa Ghilarducci's analysis—must predict layoff/death timing, save 7-30% of income, earn 3% above inflation annually, never withdraw for emergencies, time withdrawals to die with zero
- **Pay-to-Play 401(k)**: Mutual funds pay revenue-sharing to get "shelf space" on plan menu—not best funds, just highest bidders who recoup costs through higher fees
- **Plan Sponsor Fiduciary Duty**: Business owners legally liable for 401(k) plan—DOL audits resulted in 75% fines/penalties, average $600K per plan in 2013
- **17 Different 401(k) Fees**: Communication, record-keeping, investment, trustee expenses—67% of enrollees think there are no fees

## Key Concepts
- **From Defined Benefits to Defined Contributions**: Went from guaranteed lifetime income (pensions) to self-directed accounts—employees bear all market risk
- **3% Savings Insufficient**: Stanford professor John Shoven—"Can't save 3% for 30 years and expect to live 30 years in retirement with same income"
- **Bull Market Fuel**: 401(k) money flowing into market fueled '80s and '90s bull markets—mutual funds unprecedented war to manage assets
- **Dr. Alicia Munnell's Mistake**: Even Fed Reserve member made errors—cashed out pension early to "invest better", money now gone
- **Dark Pool of Assets**: Industry hides fees—"largest dark pool where nobody knows how or whose hands are getting greased"
- **Fee Disclosure Required 2012**: Three decades of 401(k) existence before service providers required by law to disclose fees on statements
- **DOL Enforcement Rampage**: Hired 1,000 enforcement officers in 2014—75% of audited plans resulted in fines/penalties

## Mental Models
- **Nuclear Fusion Analogy**: Great invention can power city (free energy) or level city (warhead)—401(k) great if used right, damaging if misused
- **Captain of Financial Ship**: Sole responsibility for investment decisions while working full-time and raising family—scary for most
- **Illusion of Control**: Markets rising made us mistake luck for being "good investor"—false confidence
- **Headwind vs Tailwind**: High-fee plans = insurmountable headwind; low-cost plans = momentum tailwind
- **Papa Bear Protecting Cubs**: Business owners must protect employees from excessive fees—legal duty as plan sponsor

## Anti-patterns
- **"401(k) Is My Retirement Plan"**: Was meant to supplement pensions, not replace—cannot finance 30-year retirement with 30-year work at 10% savings
- **"I Have No Choice"**: Employees can educate HR/management about fees—high earners have most to lose
- **"My Employer Chose This Plan"**: Business owners often clueless—broker sold expensive plan, didn't explain alternatives
- **"Fees Must Be Reasonable"**: 67% think there are no fees—plans average 2.5%+ in total costs
- **"Big Company Must Have Good Plan"**: Caterpillar, General Dynamics, Bank of America, even Fidelity sued by employees over excessive fees
