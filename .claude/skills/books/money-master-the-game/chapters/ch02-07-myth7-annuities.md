# Chapter 2.7: Myth 7: "I Hate Annuities, and You Should Too"

## Core Idea
Not all annuities are created equal—variable annuities charge 4.7% annually in fees (mutual fund costs + mortality expense + administrative), destroying wealth. But traditional income annuities and fixed indexed annuities can provide guaranteed lifetime income with 100% principal protection.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **2,000-Year Track Record**: First lifetime income annuities date to Roman Empire—citizens deposited in pool, longest-livers got increasing income, government kept cut
- **Variable Annuity Fee Disaster**: 3.1% mutual fund fees + 1.35% mortality expense + 0.25% administrative = 4.7% annually—$4,700 per $100K before making dime
- **Death Benefit Guarantee Trap**: Variable annuities guarantee heirs get original deposit if account drops—most expensive life insurance, locks investors in with surrender charges
- **David Babbel's Lifetime Income Plan**: Staggered guaranteed income annuities—100% principal protection, didn't lose in 2000 or 2008, complete peace of mind
- **Roth IRA + Annuity = Tax-Free Lifetime Income**: Combine Roth IRA with lifetime income annuity—legal tax-free lifetime income regardless of future tax rates

## Key Concepts
- **Bernanke's Largest Holdings**: Fed chairman's two largest assets were annuities—disclosed before becoming chairman
- **"I Hate Annuities" Marketing**: Active stock pickers bash annuities to sell their underperforming services—comparison strategy makes theirs look better
- **$150B Variable Annuity Market**: 2012 sales just below Apple's gross revenue—commission darling of large brokerage firms
- **Variable Annuity = Mutual Fund Wrapper**: Same underperforming mutual funds inside insurance contract for tax deferral—fees on top of fees
- **Coca-Cola Classic Annuity**: Original single version for 2,000 years—simple contract, guaranteed income/reverse, exact payment schedule
- **Evolution = Mutants**: 50 years produced many annuity types—"more poor products than good ones" (Jack Bogle)
- **Surrender Charges**: Heavy penalties for leaving variable annuities early—painted into corner by death benefit guarantee
- **Two Exceptions**: Vanguard and TIAA-CREF offer low-cost variable annuities with index funds—no commissions, no surrender charges

## Mental Models
- **Mutant vs Classic**: Original annuities simple and effective; modern "evolved" versions like variable annuities dangerous
- **Expensive Life Insurance**: Death benefit guarantee in variable annuities = most expensive life insurance available
- **Painted Into Corner**: Investors afraid to leave variable annuities due to surrender charges and death benefit
- **Paycheck for Life**: Why we invest in first place—guaranteed lifetime income stream without working
- **Grandpa's Annuities**: Traditional income annuities and fixed indexed annuities—not your grandpa's variable annuity

## Anti-patterns
- **"All Annuities Are Bad"**: Lumping all annuities together discriminates against only financial tool with 2,000-year track record
- **"Tax Deferral Justifies Fees"**: 4.7% annual fees to avoid taxes—probably won't have gains after fees to tax
- **"Death Benefit = Protection"**: Most expensive form of life insurance—locks you into underperforming investment
- **"Variable Annuities Provide Growth"**: Mutual funds inside already underperform market and charge high fees—wrapper adds more costs
- **"Guaranteed Return"**: Variable annuities don't guarantee returns, only original deposit to heirs—market risk remains

1. **4.7% Annual Fee Disaster**: Variable annuities cost $4,700 per $100K annually—3.1% mutual funds + 1.35% mortality + 0.25% administrative
