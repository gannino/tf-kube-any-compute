# Chapter 2.9: Myth 9: "The Lies We Tell Ourselves"

## Core Idea
The ultimate obstacle to financial success isn't external limitations—it's our own limiting perceptions, beliefs, and stories. 80% of success is psychology, 20% is mechanics. Breakthroughs happen in moments when we change strategy, story, and state simultaneously.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Three Steps to Breakthrough**: (1) Right Strategy—proven plan from successful people, (2) New Story—empowering beliefs replace limiting ones, (3) Peak State—emotional trigger to take massive action
- **Breakthrough Moment**: When impossible becomes possible—not taking years to change, but instant transformation; may take years to reach readiness point
- **Modeling Excellence**: Find someone who already accomplished what you want, model their behavior—"success leaves clues"
- **80/20 Success Formula**: 80% psychology (values, beliefs, emotions), 20% mechanics (strategy, tactics)
- **Self-Fulfilling Prophecy Cycle**: Limiting story → halfhearted effort → failure → reinforces belief that nothing works → cycle continues

## Key Concepts
- **Fear of Failure**: Universal human emotion—everyone fears not being enough; keeps us from executing even when we know what to do
- **Story Creation**: We create stories about why we're not successful—"not smart enough," "don't have natural talent," "outside our control"
- **Excuses vs Action**: Limiting stories produce million reasons why strategies won't work—excuses become self-fulfilling prophecies
- **Financial Stress Epidemic**: 44% of Americans report "high levels" of financial stress (AICPA)—keeps people up at night
- **Transformation in Moment**: True transformation happens instant—"I quit!", "I'm in!", "Let's begin!"—not gradual process but decision acted upon
- **Relationship/Weight/Money Parallels**: Same breakthrough principles apply across finances, relationships, health—strategy, story, state
- **Richard Branson's Dyslexia**: Empowering story—"have to work harder" vs limiting story "I'll never read"

## Mental Models
- **Threshold Moment**: Point where "Never again and no more"—frustration, anger, stress triggers breakthrough
- **Line in the Sand**: Decide new standard, commit to new goal—no going back once that moment happens
- **Modeling vs Reinventing**: Don't reinvent wheel—find proven strategies, implement exactly, get same results
- **Stories Control Emotions, Emotions Drive Behavior**: Change story → changes emotions → changes actions → changes results
- **I Can and I Will vs I Can't and I Won't**: Empowering story of possibility vs limiting story of impossibility

## Anti-patterns
- **"I Need More Information"**: Strategy already available—gyms, books, web everywhere; problem isn't knowledge
- **"I'll Never Be Rich/Thin/Happy"**: Limiting stories become self-fulfilling prophecies—belief shapes behavior
- **"It Only Works for Lucky/Rich/Talented"**: Disempowering stories blame external factors—wealthy people just did something different
- **"I Don't Have Time/Money/Education"**: Excuses prevent action—people with less resources succeeded through modeling
- **"I'm Big-Boned" (Tony's Story)**: True but unhelpful story—must change to empower weight loss

## Key Takeaways
1. **80% Psychology, 20% Mechanics**: Knowing what to do doesn't guarantee doing it—beliefs and emotions drive action
2. **Breakthroughs Are Instant**: May take years to reach readiness point, but transformation happens in moment of decision
3. **Three Elements Required**: Strategy (proven plan), Story (empowering beliefs), State (emotional trigger)
4. **Model Successful People**: Find someone who achieved what you want, do exactly what they did—"success leaves clues"
5. **Stories Control Everything**: Limiting stories produce failure; empowering stories produce success—same strategy, different results
6. **Financial Stress Affects 44%**: Nearly half of Americans report high financial stress—impacts health, relationships, decisions
7. **Change Story, Change Life**: What's wrong with your life as easy to find as what's right—new story unlocks new possibilities
8. **You Have Ability to Change**: No matter how long it's been this way, can change all in moment of real decision acted upon
