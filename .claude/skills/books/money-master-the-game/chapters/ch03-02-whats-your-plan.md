# Chapter 3.2: What's Your Plan?

## Core Idea
Everyone has different "price of dreams" numbers—from $2M to $20M—because lifestyle costs vary wildly. The "It's Your Money" app helps create a personalized financial blueprint using six questions, showing realistic timelines for reaching financial freedom levels based on your actual circumstances.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **"It's Your Money" App**: Six-question calculator that creates personalized financial blueprint—conservative assumptions with flexible adjustments for your reality
- **Three Return Scenarios**: Conservative (4%), Moderate (5.5%), Aggressive (7%) after-tax returns—builds in buffer for mistakes and future variability
- **Tax-Efficient Accumulation**: Use tax-advantaged accounts (Roth IRA, 401k, country equivalents) to generate higher net returns—Roth recommended unless certain taxes will be lower
- **Save More Tomorrow Plan**: Commit percentage of future raises to Freedom Fund—10% raise means 3% to savings, 7% to lifestyle; repeated raises can double savings rate painlessly
- **Home Value Exclusion**: Plan excludes primary home value from calculations—you always need somewhere to live; creates additional conservative cushion
- **Three Response Categories**: (1) Young and in debt—can achieve security, (2) Closer than thought—5-10 years away, (3) Started late—can still catch up with right strategy

## Key Concepts
- **Schwab 10% vs App 7%**: Market averaged 10% (1972-2012), app assumes 30% taxes → 7% after-tax; tax-deferred vehicles compound at full rate until withdrawal
- **Vanguard 4% Baseline**: Conservative return benchmark; app uses similar but with net-after-tax approach for global applicability
- **Roth Advantage**: Returns are 100% yours—7% return means keep all 7%; 10% means keep all 10%; no future tax on growth
- **Stronghold Technology Platform**: Links all investment accounts, shows actual historical returns, best/worst years, true fee impact
- **Social Security Integration**: Many neglect this future income stream; including it can dramatically shorten timeline (Angela's case: 24 years → 14 years)
- **Demographic Inevitability**: Trends that must occur—76 million baby boomers aging creates senior housing investment opportunity
- **Senior Housing REITs**: 7% income/dividend payment, tax advantages from depreciation, invest $438/month for 20 years → $228,572 generating $16,000 annual income

## Mental Models
- **Choose Your Own Adventure**: First plan is starting point; play with variables, choose different paths, improve upon it in subsequent chapters
- **Personal Financial Trainer**: Takes real numbers (savings, income), calculates future worth based on anticipated outcomes
- **Crystal Ball Forecasting**: Not about where/how to invest yet—opportunity to see what's realistic, possible, worth dreaming
- **Three Responses**: Most people fall into categories after running plan—young/debt, closer than thought, started late
- **Olympic Track Stagger**: Life's not a competition—everyone has different starting positions and definitions of success
- **Business Partial Sale Strategy**: Sell portion or majority of business while still running it—cash flow bump for freedom today + continued enjoyment
- **Overestimate Year, Underestimate Decade**: Most people overestimate annual capability, massively underestimate what's possible in 10-20 years with compounding
- **Architect Not Manager**: You're not managing circumstances, you're architecting your life's experience

## Anti-patterns
- **"It's Too Good To Be True"**: Calculator showing fast timelines feels wrong—disconnect because head number ($10-30M) had no basis in reality
- **"Need to Sell Business"**: Financial security doesn't require selling—declare victory knowing assets exist; can continue building
- **"Starting Late Means Impossible"**: Angela started at 48 with nothing—Social Security alone shaved decade off timeline
- **"Home Equity Counts"**: Counting primary home value for income generation is risky—always need somewhere to live
- **"Taxes Will Be Lower"**: Governments worldwide spent money they don't have—taxes likely rising, Roth provides certainty
- **"Can't Afford to Save More"**: Save More Tomorrow uses future raises, not current income—painless acceleration

## Key Takeaways
1. **Five Friends Example**: Wall Streeter needs $20M, photographer $10M, real estate developer $5M, wine merchant $2M, civil servant relies on pension—all happy, different numbers
2. **Marco (33, $75K, $20K debt)**: Debt-free in 7 years, Financial Security by 53, Independence by 60, Freedom by 65—started with nothing but debt
3. **Katherine (Business Owner)**: Needed $100K/year for security, already generating $300K+ profits—financially secure RIGHT NOW without selling business
4. **Angela (48, Never Saved)**: 24 years to security initially, including Social Security → 14 years to security by age 62; added senior housing REITs for independence
5. **Roth vs Traditional**: Unless certain taxes lower future, Roth wins—100% of growth yours forever, no future tax haircut
6. **Save More Tomorrow Power**: Three 10% raises with 3% to savings each → 19% savings rate, almost double starting point, all from future income
7. **Business Sale Strategy**: Can sell portion/majority of business for cash flow while still controlling it—freedom today + continued growth
8. **Compounding Decade Effect**: What seems impossible in one year becomes achievable in 10-20 years with relentless right action
