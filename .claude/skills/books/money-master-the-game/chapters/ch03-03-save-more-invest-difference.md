# Chapter 3.3: Speed It Up: 1. Save More and Invest the Difference

## Core Idea
First of five strategies to accelerate your financial plan: save more by cutting expenses that no longer bring joy and redirect those savings to compound growth. Through mortgage prepayment, vehicle optimization, the "Latte Factor," and mindful spending, you can save hundreds of thousands to millions over time.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Save More Tomorrow**: Nobel Prize winner Richard Thaler's strategy—commit percentage of future raises to savings; blue-collar workers went from "can't save" to 14-19% savings rate in 5 years
- **Banker's Secret**: 80% of 30-year mortgage payments go to interest; interest doubles cost of home over life of loan; prepay next month's principal to cut mortgage in half
- **Money Power Principle 3**: Cut mortgage payments in half—write second check for next month's principal-only portion; $270K home at 6% saves $270K in interest
- **The Latte Factor**: David Bach's metaphor for small, mindless purchases that compound into massive losses; $4/day coffee = $56.5K over 20 years at 6%
- **Mindful Savings Exercise**: Six-step process to identify, evaluate, and eliminate expenses that no longer bring joy relative to financial freedom
- **34.5% Interest Expense**: Average American spends 34.5% of take-home pay on interest (mortgage + credit cards + auto loans)—huge savings opportunity
- **Spending Plan vs Budget**: Not about restriction, but planning spending to maximize joy while ensuring long-term financial freedom
- **Online Rewards Programs**: Upromise, Ebates, Extrabux save 10-30% on purchases; Lisa's husband found better prices 80% of the time

## Key Concepts
- **Prepay Principal Strategy**: Traditional fixed-rate mortgage—write second check for next month's principal-only portion; $270 extra on $270K loan at 6% cuts 30-year mortgage to 15 years, saves ~$270K
- **80-90% Early Interest**: First years of mortgage, 80-90% of payment is interest anyway; Americans move/refinance every 5-7 years and restart cycle
- **BMW X6 Example**: $1,200/month lease payment = entire house payment; trading down freed cash for home purchase
- **Angela's Car Trade**: Traded old car for new, saved $400/month ($4,800/year) to invest toward financial freedom
- **Bottled Water Cost**: $150/month ($1,800/year) on Smartwater × 40 years at 8% = $503,605—literally pissing away half million dollars
- **Coffee Math**: $4/day × 20 years at 6% = $56,500; $10/day (2-3 visits) = $141,250—cost of four-year college education
- **Pizza vs Restaurant**: $40/week savings from ordering in = $2,080/year; at 8% over 40 years = $503,000+
- **Lisa's Impulse Purchase Rule**: Photo "must-have" items, give husband two weeks to find online; 80% of time found 20-30% cheaper

## Mental Models
- **Extra Time Gift**: Finishing work early gives bonus time; finding 2-5 years of financial savings is lifetime bonus
- **Not Deprivation, Adjustment**: Don't deprive yourself—adjust spending to mirror core values, indulge only what truly matters
- **Deliberate Spending**: Choose how to allocate funds for biggest bang; still enjoy finer pleasures but in control
- **Transportation vs Status**: BMW story—car was ego stroke, not transportation; joy shifted to home/family priorities
- **Timing Over Lifestyle**: Simple changes today insure more than enough later to fund lifestyle and dreams
- **Compounding Multiplier**: Small daily savings compound to surprising giant numbers over decades
- **Joy Comparison**: Rate expenses 0-10 for pleasure; compare to feeling of Absolute Financial Freedom
- **Story Change**: Can't squeeze water from rock, but can change belief about saving capacity

## Anti-patterns
- **"Can't Possibly Save More"**: Blue-collar workers said same, Save More Tomorrow proved otherwise—5 years to 14-19% savings
- **"Need Lower Interest Rate"**: Don't need rate cut to save—prepay principal strategy works regardless of rate environment
- **"Need Double Payments"**: Prepaying next month's principal is tiny fraction; $270 on $1,618 payment cuts mortgage in half
- **"It's Just Coffee"**: Small daily mindless purchases compound to massive wealth destruction over decades
- **"Budgets Suck"**: Agreed—spending plans, not budgets; plan for joy plus freedom
- **"Depriving Myself"**: Not deprivation when expenses no longer bring current joy; redirect to future freedom

## Key Takeaways
1. **Five Speed Strategies**: (1) Save more/invest difference, (2) Earn more/invest difference, (3) Get better returns, (4) Pay less in taxes, (5) Change environment
2. **30-Year Mortgage Reality**: 80% of payments go to interest; interest doubles home cost; $500K home → $1M total; $1M home → $2M+
3. **Principal Prepayment Power**: $270 extra on $270K 6% mortgage = cut 30-year loan to 15 years, save ~$270K in interest
4. **Average Interest Burden**: 34.5% of take-home pay to interest (mortgage + credit cards + auto)—huge target for savings
5. **Latte Factor Examples**: $4/day coffee = $56.5K over 20 years; $10/day = $141.25K (college education cost); $1,800/year water = $503K over 40 years
6. **BMW vs Freedom**: $1,200/month car payment = house payment amount; trade down frees cash for real assets
7. **Mindful Savings Six Steps**: (1) Brainstorm recurring expenses, (2) Calculate costs, (3) Rate joy 0-10, (4) Visualize financial freedom feeling, (5) Compare priorities, (6) Commit to three eliminations
8. **Lisa's 80-20 Rule**: Photo impulse purchases, two-week online search period; 80% of time found 20-30% cheaper—$100s saved per purchase
