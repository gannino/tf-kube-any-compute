# Chapter 3.4: Speed It Up: 2. Earn More and Invest the Difference

## Core Idea
Second speed strategy: increase your value in the marketplace to earn more. The economy pays for value, not time—by becoming more valuable through skills, creativity, and service to others, you can multiply your income without limits.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Become More Valuable**: Jim Rohn's core principle—"We get paid for bringing value to the marketplace...don't wish it was easier, wish you were better"
- **Invest in Yourself**: Warren Buffett's most powerful investment—Dale Carnegie course changed his life; personal development pays highest returns
- **Work Harder on Yourself Than Your Job**: Develop skills, creativity, and capacity to serve more people—salary becomes unlimited
- **Serve Many to Earn Many**: Biblical principle—become servant of many; serve millions, earn millions; law of added value
- **Income Ladder Reality**: $7.25/hour ($15,080/year) minimum to $3.5 billion annual income (David Tepper)—difference is value added
- **Value Multiplier Math**: 42% return vs 0.33% bank return = 12,627% more economic value delivered; marketplace pays proportionately
- **Retool or Be Obsolete**: 47% of occupations at risk of automation within 20 years (Oxford study); must add value at higher level
- **Unemployment by Income**: Great Recession—10% overall unemployment, but <1% for $100K+ earners; valuable skills always needed
- **Get in Front of Trends**: Nick Woodman (GoPro), Sara Blakely (Spanx)—wealth goes to those positioning ahead of technological/cultural shifts

## Key Concepts
- **Jim Rohn Seminar Investment**: $35 (one week's pay) for seminar condensed 20 years of learning into days—transformed Tony's life trajectory
- **We Don't Get Paid for Time**: Market pays for value delivered, not hours worked; same time, vastly different compensation based on value
- **South Korean Teacher Kim Ki-hoon**: Earned $4 million annually—$4/hour online classes, 60-hour weeks (3 hours lecturing, 57 hours improving); served thousands vs classroom of 30
- **Daniela's Four-Job Strategy**: Did work of four people, proved she could save company 50% of marketing costs, negotiated double salary
- **Mike Rowe Skills Gap**: 3.5 million jobs available, only 10% require four-year degree—90% need training/skills/willingness to learn
- **Technology Disruption Pattern**: 1860s—80% farmers, today 2% feed world; new jobs emerge at higher income levels for those who adapt
- **$500-3000/month Multiplier**: Extra $500/month invested at 8% for 40 years = $1.5M; $1,000/month = $3M; $3,000/month = $9M
- **Silicon Valley Jobs**: Can't fill positions fast enough—technology companies desperate for qualified workers; skills gap, not job shortage
- **Spanx Zero-Outside Investment**: Sara Blakely owned 100%, zero debt, never took outside funding—$5K initial investment, wrote own patent from textbook

## Mental Models
- **Ladder to Climb**: America's income system—starts at minimum, no ceiling for those who add massive value
- **Chicken Feed**: If you help company make $1B, paying $52M is "not that much money"—value justifies compensation
- **Raise Effective When You Employ Yourself**: Entrepreneurs don't wait for boss—give raise immediately when delivering more value
- **Problems vs Opportunities**: Always available—focus on problems = struggle; focus on opportunities = growth
- **Employ Yourself or Get Employed**: If truly valuable with needed skills, someone will pay you regardless of economy
- **Circular History**: 1978 interest rates skyrocketed to 18%; recessions come and go; valuable skills always in demand
- **Pivot When Obsolete**: Silicon Valley term—change direction after failure; old jobs not coming back, must retool
- **Marketplace Always Tells True Worth**: Ki-hoon's $4M teaching salary proved value; Daniela's double pay confirmed worth

## Anti-patterns
- **"Economy Too Tough"**: 2008 lost 8.8M jobs, but <$1% unemployment for $100K+ earners—valuable skills always needed
- **"Old Jobs Coming Back"**: Most not returning; must retool for new marketplace or be obsolete
- **"Profession Limits Me"**: Kim Ki-hoon proved teacher can earn $4M—limitation is creativity, not job title
- **"Need Four-Year Degree"**: Mike Rowe—90% of 3.5M available jobs don't require degree; need skills/training/willingness
- **"Just Janitor"**: Tony started as janitor, $40/week—invested in self, became multi-millionaire through value-added service
- **"Not Entrepreneur"**: Daniela corporate employee, added value by doing four jobs, doubled salary without starting business
- **"Need Luck or Timing"**: Kim Ki-hoon, Nick Woodman, Sara Blakely succeeded through relentless self-improvement, not chance
- **"Can't Afford Education"**: Tony invested week's pay ($35) for life-changing seminar; best ROI ever

4. **42% vs 0.33% Return**: Tepper delivered 12,627% more economic value than bank; proportional compensation follows
5. **Kim Ki-hoon $4M Teacher**: $4/hour online × thousands of students = rock star teacher; worked 57 hours improving, 3 lecturing
6. **Daniela's Double**: Did four jobs, saved company 50% marketing costs, proved value → double salary through negotiation
7. **Technology Disruption**: 47% jobs at risk automation (Oxford); 1860s 80% farmers → 2% today feed world at higher income
8. **$500-3000/month Power**: Extra $500/month invested 40 years at 8% = $1.5M; $1K = $3M; $3K = $9M nest egg
9. **Get Front of Trends**: GoPro (digital photography + social sharing), Spanx (new shapewear category)—wealth from positioning
10. **Skills Gap Opportunity**: 3.5M jobs available, 90% don't require degree—training/skills/willingness to learn path to $100K+
