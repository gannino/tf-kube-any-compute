## Asset Allocation Quick Guide

### Three-Bucket System

| Bucket | Purpose | Typical Allocation | Key Investments |
|--------|---------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Security** | Lifetime income, capital preservation | 40-60% | Bonds, annuities, CDs, pensions |
| **Risk/Growth** | Long-term growth, beat inflation | 30-50% | Stocks, REITs, growth funds |
| **Dream** | Enjoy life, fulfill aspirations | 5-10% | Higher-risk opportunities |

### Age-Based Allocation Rule of Thumb

| Age | Security Bucket | Risk/Growth Bucket | Dream Bucket |
|-----|----------------|-------------------|--------------|
| 20s-30s | 30-40% | 50-60% | 5-10% |
| 40s-50s | 40-50% | 40-50% | 5-10% |
| 60s+ | 50-60% | 30-40% | 5-10% |

## Economic Seasons Guide

| Season | Characteristics | Winning Assets | Losing Assets |
|--------|----------------|----------------|---------------|
| **Growth** | Rising growth, moderate inflation | Stocks, Commodities | Cash, Bonds |
| **Inflation** | Rising inflation, stagnant growth | Gold, Commodities, TIPS | Bonds, Cash |
| **Deflation** | Falling prices, economic contraction | Long-term Treasuries | Stocks, Commodities |
| **Recession** | Falling growth, falling inflation | Intermediate-term Treasuries | Stocks, Commodities |

## Tax-Advantaged Accounts Priority Order

1. **401(k) with employer match** — Free money, contribute up to match
2. **Roth IRA** — Tax-free growth and withdrawals
3. **Traditional IRA** — Tax-deferred growth
4. **401(k) beyond match** — If low fees and good options
5. **HSA (Health Savings Account)** — Triple tax advantage if eligible
6. **Taxable brokerage account** — With tax-efficient funds

## Decision Matrix: Active vs. Passive Investing

| Factor | Choose Active If... | Choose Passive If... |
|--------|---------------------|---------------------|
| **Time** | You enjoy researching and monitoring | You want set-and-forget |
| **Expertise** | You have deep knowledge and access | You're not a financial professional |
| **Performance** | You can consistently beat fees + taxes | You accept market returns |
| **Fees** | You accept 2%+ total costs | You want <0.5% total costs |
| **Taxes** | You actively manage tax efficiency | You prefer automatic tax efficiency |
| **Evidence** | You believe you're in the 4% who beat market | You accept 96% of active funds fail |

## Common Financial Pitfalls to Avoid

| Pitfall | Why It's Dangerous | Solution |
|---------|-------------------|----------|
| **Chasing past performance** | Winners become losers (reversion to mean) | Systematic approach, index funds |
| **Market timing** | Impossible to do consistently | Dollar-cost averaging, stay invested |
| **High fees** | Compound negatively over decades | Low-cost index funds (<0.5%) |
| **Home country bias** | Japan 1990s lesson | Global diversification |
| **Inadequate diversification** | "Balanced" portfolios are 95% stocks | True risk-based allocation |
| **Ignoring taxes** | Biggest expense in life | Tax-advantaged accounts first |
| **Lifestyle inflation** | Never reach financial freedom | Save/invest raises, not spend |
| ** emotional selling** | Locks in losses | Have plan, stick to it |
