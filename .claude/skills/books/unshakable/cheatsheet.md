# Unshakeable Cheatsheet

## Core Four Quick Reference

| Principle | Key Question | Action Step | Red Flag |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|-----------|
| **Don't Lose** | What's the downside? | Avoid permanent capital loss | Investments that could go to zero |
| **Asymmetric Risk/Reward** | Is it 5-to-1? | Risk $1 to make $5 | Chasing upside without limiting downside |
| **Tax Efficiency** | What's the after-tax return? | Use asset location strategy | Ignoring tax impact of decisions |
| **Diversification** | Across 4 levels? | Asset classes, within classes, countries, time | Concentrated bets |

## 7 Questions to Ask Any Advisor

1. Are you a Registered Investment Advisor? ✅ YES required
2. Are you affiliated with a broker-dealer? ❌ NO required (if yes, run!)
3. Do you offer proprietary products? ❌ NO required
4. Do you receive third-party compensation? ❌ NO required
5. What's your investing philosophy? Should NOT claim stock-picking beats market
6. What planning services beyond investing? Tax, estate, college planning
7. Where will my money be held? Third-party custodian (Fidelity/Schwab) — NOT advisor

**Red Flag**: If you see "Securities offered through [company], member FINRA and SIPC" → RUN!

## Bear Market Playbook

### Before the Crash (Preparation)
- [ ] Write down why you own each asset class
- [ ] Understand historical bear market performance
- [ ] Know your advisor's crisis strategy
- [ ] Have 5+ years expenses in conservative investments

### During the Crash (Discipline)
- [ ] Review your written plan
- [ ] Remember: Every bear → bull (no exceptions in US history)
- [ ] Don't check portfolio daily
- [ ] Use dollar-cost averaging to buy more shares lower
- [ ] Consider tax-loss harvesting opportunities

### After the Crash (Recovery)
- [ ] Stay invested — missing best days devastates returns
- [ ] Rebalance to target allocations (buy low, sell high)
- [ ] Review lessons learned
- [ ] Update plan if circumstances changed

## Psychology Cheat Sheet

### Suffering Triggers (Recognize to Stop)
| Trigger | Pattern | Antidote |
|---------|---------|----------|
| **Loss** | "I've lost/will lose X" | Appreciate what you have |
| **Less** | "I'll have less X" | Focus on abundance |
| **Never** | "I'll never have X" | Remember: never say never |

### 90-Second Rule (Emergency Stop)
1. **Catch yourself** when tension rises
2. **Breathe deeply** and slow down
3. **Step back** from thoughts (don't believe them)
4. **Appreciate** something (breath, heartbeat, being alive)

### Gratitude Meditation (2-Minute Reset)
1. Hands on heart, close eyes, breathe deep
2. Feel grateful for your heart (gift you didn't earn)
3. Recall 3 grateful experiences (step into them)
4. Feel gratitude for this moment
5. Result: Heart-brain coherence, beautiful state

## Red Flags 🚩

- **FINRA/SIPC affiliation** → Broker, not fiduciary
- **Proprietary mutual funds** → Conflict of interest
- **"Beats the market" claims** → Probably lying or lucky
- **High fees (1%+)** -> Destroying your wealth
- **"This time is different"** → Sir John Templeton's 4 most expensive words
- **Cash is "safe"** → Losing to inflation + opportunity cost
- **"I'll wait for clarity"** → Clarity comes too late; opportunity gone
- **"I can time the market"** → Must be right 69-91% of time (impossible)

## Green Flags ✅

- **Registered Investment Advisor** → Fiduciary duty
- **Third-party custodian** (Fidelity/Schwab) → Your money is safe
- **Low-cost index funds** (0.05-0.2%) → Keeping more of your returns
- **Tax-efficient strategies** → Focus on after-tax returns
- **Written investment plan** → Discipline over emotion
- **Diversified across asset classes** → Reduced volatility
- **Long-term perspective** (5+ years) → Time in market > timing market
- **Education-focused advisor** → Teaches you to be independent
